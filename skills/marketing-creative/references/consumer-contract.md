# Creative consumer contract

Source: WOIA Real Estate docs/21 existing-provider compatibility (Creative, Marketing + Ads), docs/22 provenance/source authority, docs/24 authority/effects, docs/25 engineering gates and docs/26 marketplace consumer graph.

Accepted handoff carries consumer `Marketing` or `Ads`, operation `brief`, `design`, `qa` or `variant`, an immutable source reference, and generation qualification only when generation is requested. No publication, person-contact, paid or financial effect is authorized. The guard validates an authoring handoff; it does not enforce an external adapter or constitute business-fact acceptance.
